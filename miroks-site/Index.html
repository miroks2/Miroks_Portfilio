About me

3 years of 3D craftsmanship

From blockout to final polish — I handle the full pipeline.

Hi, I'm Miroks 👋

I'm a 3D builder & modeler specializing in Roblox Studio environments. For over 3 years I've been turning ideas into playable 3D worlds — from cozy interiors to large-scale stylized landscapes.

I work fast, clean and in any known art style — realistic, semi-realistic, stylized, low-poly, hand-painted, sci-fi, fantasy. Whatever the project needs — I match the reference and deliver on time.

My main tool is Blender: modeling, sculpting, UV-unwrapping and — when the project needs it — painting textures by hand in Texture Paint. No generic asset packs. Every texture is made for the specific project.
